Categories

A category of your own is a kind of entry beside the built-in ones. Put it under another kind and it is a subcategory, listed and folded under that kind's entries the way maneuvers sit under a discipline. Categories are kept in this browser and travel in a project export (Import / Export → Download project), so another workbench reads them in with the entries. Save before choosing a new category as another's parent. Removing a category keeps its entries, shown as articles.
